The Local Coin — Community Currency & Debit Card

Inspired by Bristol Pound (UK), BerkShares (MA), and MiniPay/Visa (Global). A closed-loop local economy tool that keeps capital circulating within Trifecta Zones.

💳 Visa-Branded Debit Card

White-label Visa card powered by Gnosis Pay infrastructure. Apple Pay / Google Pay compatible. 0% merchant fees for participating vendors (vs. 1.5–3.5% for credit cards). Chargeback protection included.

🏦 Credit Union Backend

Partner with Oregon Community Credit Union or OnPoint. Every Local Coin backed 1:1 by USD held in reserve. Electronic accounts managed by regulated institution — same model as Bristol Pound + Bristol Credit Union.

📱 Mobile App + Dashboard

Consumer side: balance, deposits, purchase history, "My Community Impact" score. Vendor side: monthly sales, top items, customer demographics. Both sides see real-time transaction flow.

🎁 5% Employer Match Bonus

When employer deposits Track A/B/C payouts into Local Coin, they add 5% bonus. $5,000 profit share → $5,250 in Local Coin. Bonus is tax-deductible recruitment expense. Employee sees immediate 5% upside.

🏠 Down Payment Bridge

Local Coin balance counts toward OHCS Flex Lending "qualified down payment source." App shows: "Your balance: $X. You are $Y from your FirstHome down payment goal!" Gamifies wealth-building.

🌾 Farmers Market Network

Participating vendors get "Trifecta Preferred" badge. Cardholders get $25 bonus after spending $500 locally. Vendors can see branded card at checkout, reinforcing community identity. Backend tracks all vendor sales monthly.
